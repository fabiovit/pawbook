# 🐾 PawBook v1.1.3

This maintenance release fixes the visual parent-child alignment in the fourth pedigree generation.

## Fixed

- Groups every fourth-generation father and mother directly beneath their correct child in the third generation.
- RED and ADELAIDE now appear beneath MOMO DELLA CASCINETTA.
- ROMEO and MILA now appear beneath BEATRICE DELLA METAURENSE.
- The same relationship is preserved for all eight ancestor pairs.
- Added visible pair connectors to make each family branch immediately understandable.
- Kept fourth-generation cards compact and clickable for complete ancestor details.
- Preserved Italian `DD/MM/YYYY` date formatting, the green **Vivo** status, dog photo support and ENCI data.

## Updating

Restart Home Assistant completely after installing the update, then refresh the PawBook page.
