# PawBook v6.2.0 – Visual Redesign

PawBook 6.2.0 completes the visual redesign started with the DOMOTICA/Inverter navigation shell.

## What changed

The interface is no longer visually built as a stack of independent rounded cards.

- New application-style pet profile hero.
- Unified telemetry rail for weight, vaccination, visit and age.
- Flatter health sections with subtle separators instead of heavy card shadows.
- Smart Dashboard rebuilt as a single coherent data grid.
- Cleaner lists for vaccinations, visits, treatments, heat cycles and health timeline.
- Reduced nested card appearance in ENCI and Multi-Pet sections.
- More compact geometry on desktop and mobile.

## Preserved

- DOMOTICA/Inverter header and menu
- Multi-Pet Hub
- Smart Health
- Vaccination Center
- Veterinary Center
- Treatments Center
- Weight Center
- Heat Cycle Center
- Health Calendar and automatic reminders
- ENCI Pro and genealogy
- HACS / Hassfest workflows
- Ko-fi support
