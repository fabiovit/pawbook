# PawBook v6.10.6 – Dark Theme Fix

PawBook v6.10.6 restores the approved dark-theme appearance while preserving the light-theme improvements introduced in v6.10.5.

## What changed

- Restores the original deep dark palette used by the approved PawBook Health Control Center.
- Restores the black/dark header and navigation appearance.
- Restores dark hero surfaces, management modules and health cards.
- Improves contrast in dark mode.
- Keeps the teal PawBook accent unchanged.
- Preserves the v6.10.5 light-theme rendering.
- No layout, navigation, data or functionality changes.
- Keeps the stable `pawbook-panel-v630.js` frontend base.

## Update notes

After updating, restart Home Assistant once and perform a forced browser refresh if the previous frontend remains cached.
