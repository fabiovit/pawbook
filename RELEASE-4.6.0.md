# PawBook v4.6.0 – Heat Cycle Center

PawBook 4.6.0 transforms heat-cycle tracking into a dedicated dashboard.

## Highlights

- Complete heat-cycle history.
- Duration shown for every completed cycle.
- Average recorded duration.
- Median interval between recorded cycles.
- Estimated next heat date calculated from the individual dog's history.
- Probable date window adjusted using the variability of previous intervals.
- Confidence indicator based on the amount of available history.
- Clear notice that the estimate is statistical and not a veterinary prediction.
- Responsive smartphone layout.
- Existing heat records remain fully editable and compatible.
- Includes the frontend registration fix from v4.5.1.

The next-heat estimate requires at least two recorded cycle start dates and becomes more useful as the history grows.
