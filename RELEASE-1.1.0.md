# 🐾 PawBook v1.1.0

PawBook now supports a locally stored dog photo directly from the Home Assistant panel.

## New

- Upload a dog photo from desktop or mobile.
- Automatic centered square crop and resize to 640×640 px.
- Preview before saving.
- Replace or remove the saved photo at any time.
- The same photo is displayed in the dog profile and pedigree header.
- Supports JPG, PNG and WebP source images.

## Privacy and storage

- The resized photo is stored locally inside PawBook data.
- No external image hosting or cloud service is required.
- Existing health records, ENCI data and genealogy are preserved.

## Updating

Restart Home Assistant completely after installing the update.
Then open PawBook and click the photo area or camera badge to add the dog's photo.
