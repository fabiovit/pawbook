# PawBook v5.0.0 – Health Hub

PawBook 5.0 focuses on clarity, automation and interaction across the health dashboard.

## Automatic Reminders

Reminders are generated automatically from the data already stored in PawBook. There is nothing to fill in manually.

The new Reminders Center can surface:

- vaccination recalls that are expired or due within 30 days;
- treatments ending within 3 days;
- veterinary visits when more than 12 months have passed;
- weight reminders when more than 30 days have passed since the last measurement;
- entry into the estimated next heat-cycle window.

The reminder strip on the Smart Dashboard is now fully clickable and opens the complete Reminders Center.

## Heat Cycle Center fixes

- Fixed Modify / Delete buttons that could appear over unrelated cards.
- Heat-cycle controls are now positioned inside the correct cycle row.
- Clicking a heat-cycle row opens that exact cycle for editing.
- The edit window clearly displays the selected cycle start and end dates.
- Delete confirmation now identifies the exact heat cycle being removed.
- Record matching is more robust.

## Preserved features

- Smart Dashboard
- Health Calendar
- Estimated next heat cycle
- Compact and filterable Health Timeline
- Vaccination Center
- Veterinary Center
- Treatments Center
- Weight Center
- ENCI Pro
