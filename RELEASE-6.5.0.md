# PawBook v6.5.0 – Navigation & Layout Polish

PawBook 6.5.0 refines Health OS with clearer navigation and better spacing.

## Navigation

The top bar now keeps only Panoramica, Smart Health, Agenda, Salute and ENCI visible. A new **Gestione** hub groups Peso, Vaccini, Visite, Terapie, Calori, Genealogia, Statistiche, Documenti, Report and Backup.

On desktop Gestione opens as a structured panel. On mobile it becomes a compact touch-friendly management view.

## Visual polish

- Removed the cyan vertical rails from section pages.
- Added more breathing room around headings and content.
- Improved section hierarchy without adding more card-like decoration.
- Refined Smart Health rows and grouped-navigation active states.
- Reduced the feeling of content being attached to the left edge of the screen.

## Preserved

Health Command Center, Health Agenda, Multi-Pet Hub, ENCI Pro, genealogy, documents, reports, backups and all existing health records remain available.
