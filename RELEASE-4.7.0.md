# PawBook v4.7.0 – Smart Dashboard & Health Timeline

PawBook 4.7.0 makes the home screen more useful and the health history much easier to read.

## Smart Dashboard

The PawBook home now brings together the most useful information for the selected pet:

- overall Smart Health status;
- next vaccination;
- latest veterinary visit;
- active treatments;
- current weight and latest variation;
- estimated next heat cycle and probable window.

Every dashboard card links directly to its detailed PawBook section.

## Health Timeline

The old long Health Timeline has been redesigned as a compact chronology.

- Shows the eight most recent matching events by default.
- Filters for vaccinations, visits, treatments, weight and heat cycles.
- “Show all” expands the history only when needed.
- Cleaner layout on desktop and smartphone.

## Fixes

- The PawBook header now correctly displays version **4.7.0** instead of the old hardcoded **4.4**.
- Frontend and Home Assistant panel registration are aligned on the new `pawbook-panel-v470` asset.
- Existing PawBook records and all previous centers remain compatible.
